## About Us

BRACU Shikari is a community built around cybersecurity, ethical hacking, and the pursuit of technical excellence.

We are here to **build, train, and inspire the next generation of cybersecurity enthusiasts at BRACU**. Through Capture The Flag competitions, workshops, training sessions, seminars, and community-driven initiatives, BRACU Shikari aims to make cybersecurity more accessible, structured, and exciting for everyone — from complete beginners to skilled players.

Our mission is to represent BRAC University on national and international stages while cultivating **technical excellence, ethical responsibility, and a strong cybersecurity culture** on campus and beyond.

This is just the beginning of our journey toward building a community of ethical hackers, researchers, professionals, and defenders of the digital world.

> **Hunt with purpose. Hack with ethics. Defend with skill.**

## 🏆 What We Do

- 🎯 **Capture The Flag** — Compete in CTFs across web, cryptography, pwn, reverse engineering, forensics, OSINT, and more.
- 🧠 **Training & Workshops** — Learn and share practical cybersecurity knowledge.
- 🔬 **Research & Exploration** — Explore new techniques, technologies, and areas of information security.
- 🎓 **Community Building** — Create an accessible environment for students interested in cybersecurity, from beginners to experienced players.
- 🌍 **Competitive Representation** — Represent BRAC University in national and international cybersecurity competitions.
- 🛡️ **Ethical Hacking** — Develop offensive security skills with an emphasis on responsibility, ethics, and real-world impact.
